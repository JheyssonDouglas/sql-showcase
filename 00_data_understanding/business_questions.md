# Business Questions

The analytical questions this project answers, organized by topic.

---

## Revenue

1. What is the monthly and yearly revenue trend?
2. Which states generate the most revenue, and what is their market share?
3. Which product categories drive the most revenue?
4. What is the average order value overall and by payment type?
5. What is the year-over-year revenue growth rate?

---

## Customers

6. How many unique customers exist in the dataset?
7. How many new customers are acquired each month?
8. What percentage of customers make a repeat purchase?
9. What is the estimated Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)?
10. How can customers be segmented using the RFM model?

---

## Sellers

11. Which sellers generate the most revenue?
12. What is the relationship between seller volume and review score?
13. Which sellers have the best and worst on-time delivery rates?
14. How are sellers distributed geographically?

---

## Operations & Delivery

15. What is the overall on-time delivery rate?
16. Which states have the best and worst delivery performance?
17. How many days on average does a delivery take from order to delivery?
18. How many orders were canceled, and what share is that of total orders?

---

## Product & Category

19. Which product categories have the highest and lowest average review scores?
20. What is the distribution of review scores across the platform?
21. Which categories have the highest freight cost relative to product price?

---

## Analytics Engineering

22. How can the raw Olist tables be modeled into a Star Schema?
23. What would a `fact_sales` table look like at order-item grain?
24. How can a date dimension be generated from the order date range?
